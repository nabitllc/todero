## Solume (solume.ai / community.solume.com)

- **Launched Nov 2025** — Atlanta-based AI-powered HOA and condo association management platform. No news from the last 7 days specifically, but the company is active and recently established.
- **Core product positioning:** AI assistant for governing documents (CC&Rs, bylaws, state laws via plain-English Q&A), AI-driven reserve study management, budgeting tools, ARC/compliance workflows, member records, and communication tools.
- **Reserve Study feature:** Marketed as turning static reserve studies into a "live, up-to-the-minute financial picture" connected to budgets and vendor data — pitched as a cost-saver vs. traditional reserve study firms.
- **AI Assistant:** Positioned to answer plain-English questions about community rules directly from governing documents — saving attorney fees. Also centralizes member records and board decision tracking.
- **No funding announcements found.** Appears self-funded/bootstrapped at this stage.
- **No product updates or news in the last 7 days.** Most recent press releases date to November 2025 (initial launch and reserve study feature launch).
- **Direct competitor to Kemuni** — AI-first, board-focused, similar messaging around simplifying management and reducing legal/admin overhead.

## Vantaca (vantaca.com)

- **March 12, 2026:** Eclipse Community Management (Dayton, OH) went live on Vantaca — 175 associations, 20,000+ homes. Cited full Vantaca stack: Vantaca Pay, Vantaca IQ, HOAi (AI agent), and Vantaca Home homeowner portal.
- **HOAi (AI agent):** Positioned as "agentic AI" that executes defined workflows autonomously — invoice processing, homeowner inquiry triage, routine communications, real-time status updates, ARC, ownership transfers, GL coding. Can take phone calls and resolve issues.
- **Vantaca IQ:** Deep data analytics and benchmarking layer. Cited case studies: HOA Strategies saved 800+ hours via AI, Lang Management boosted portfolio capacity 53%.
- **November 2025 (Vision Conference):** Unveiled Vantaca IQ Insights & Benchmarking, Vantaca Home with HOAi Voice for residents, and Vantaca Vendor module.
- **July 2025:** Acquired HOAi company to integrate AI capabilities natively.
- **Positioning:** "AI-first community association management platform" — clearly targeting professional management companies (not self-managed HOAs). Described as the only platform built on a "system-of-action" model.
- **No funding news in last 7 days.** Already an established, well-funded platform.
- **Active content marketing** — publishing HOA software evaluation guides and comparison content aggressively in 2026.

- **No major news or product updates found in the last 7 days.**

- **Review sentiment (2026 aggregators):** Generally positive — praised for simplicity and support team. No pricing changes found.
- **Positioning:** Self-managed HOA focus. Competes more directly with simpler tools than with Vantaca or AppFolio.

## Key Takeaways

- **AI is the battleground.** Vantaca (HOAi agentic AI) and Solume (AI assistant + reserve study AI) are both leaning hard into AI as a differentiator. AppFolio has Realm-X. Buildium and PayHOA are behind on AI messaging.
- **Vantaca is executing aggressively** — landing large management companies (20,000+ homes), publishing heavy content, and showcasing AI ROI with specific numbers (800+ hours saved, 53% capacity increase). The main enterprise threat.
- **Solume is Kemuni's closest direct comp** — same target (board-managed communities), same AI angle, launched Nov 2025 in Atlanta. No funding found; likely early-stage. Worth monitoring closely.
- **PayHOA has $27.5M to spend** (Series A, May 2024) and is building a mobile app — expect more product velocity soon. Currently the simplest option for self-managed HOAs.
- **AppFolio is deepening its ecosystem** via marketplace partnerships (AvidXchange AP automation) rather than building everything in-house — signals a platform/API strategy that could raise switching costs for mid-market customers.
