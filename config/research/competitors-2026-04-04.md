## Buildium

- **Ownership:** Owned by RealPage (part of Roper Technologies family) — provides stability/resources but not an independent startup.
  - Source: https://thepropertyceo.com/compare/buildium-review
- **Pricing (2026):** Three tiers — Essential (~$62/mo), Growth, Premium (~$400/mo). Growth/Premium include unlimited eSignatures, waived EFT/ACH fees, and robust analytics. Premium required for API integrations.
  - Source: https://www.b2breviews.com/reviews/buildium/ and https://thepropertyceo.com/compare/buildium-review
- **Recent complaint (Capterra, March 2026):** Customer reported "no-notice integration removal, blocked in-app upgrades, and shifting phone-only pricing" — described as feeling like "a forced upsell rather than a customer-first solution."
  - Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/47428/Buildium-Property-Management-Software/reviews/
- **Positioning:** Mid-market sweet spot for 50–1,500 doors. Integrates with Yardi, AppFolio, RealPage, Entrata via third-party tools.
- **No major product launches or announcements** found in the last 7 days. No new AI features publicly announced.
- **Reviewer consensus 2026:** Solid for core PM functions; complaints cluster around pricing/upsell practices and weak API access unless on Premium.
