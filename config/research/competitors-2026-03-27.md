## Solume (solume.ai)

- **Product positioning:** Markets itself as "One platform for every HOA task. Powered by AI." Core pitch is AI-automated community management — automated notices, vendor procurement, compliance via governing document AI, and financial transparency.
- **AI-first differentiation:** Solume highlights an "AI Assistant" feature that helps board members make confident legal/compliance decisions without needing to personally interpret governing documents.
- **Target market:** Self-managed HOA and condo associations (board members, not professional CAM companies). Positions against complexity and hidden fees of incumbents.
- **No major product announcements found in the last 7 days.** Website appears stable/existing product, no press releases or funding news detected.
- **Competitive note:** Closest direct AI-first competitor to Kemuni in the SMB/self-managed HOA space.

## PayHOA

- **No significant product announcements found in the last 7 days.**
- **Pricing (current, from review sites):** Tiered flat-rate model — $49/mo (≤50 units), $99/mo (51–99 units), $179/mo (≤300 units). Pricing reportedly increased recently per user reviews.
- **Recent review sentiment:** Generally positive for value/price. Recurring complaints: (1) additional transaction fees on top of subscription, (2) manual account balance refresh issues, (3) credits not posting correctly before payments (Cash Basis accounting bug reported to support).
- **Market position:** Budget-friendly option for small, self-managed HOAs. No AI features surfaced.
- **Competitive note:** Lower price point than most competitors but limited feature depth. Pricing increase recently noted by users may create churn opportunity.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Vantaca is the most active this week** — closed a 20,000-home enterprise deal, hired senior leadership, and is publishing aggressive conversion content targeting competitors' customers. They are clearly in growth mode and positioning as the premium AI-first CAM platform for professional management companies.

2. **AI is the dominant battleground** — Vantaca, AppFolio (Realm-X), Buildium, and Entrata are all racing to claim "AI-first" positioning. Entrata's 100-agent platform announcement this week raises the stakes significantly. Solume is Kemuni's closest direct AI-HOA competitor in the self-managed space.

3. **PayHOA pricing increase + bug complaints = opportunity** — PayHOA recently raised prices, and users are surfacing accounting bugs and UX friction in reviews. Their self-managed HOA customers (small boards, budget-conscious) are exactly Kemuni's target market and may be open to switching if Kemuni can offer a cleaner experience at a competitive price.

4. **Buildium/AppFolio are not core HOA competitors** — Both serve residential rental primarily; HOA is secondary. Their complexity and pricing may deter small self-managed HOAs — a positioning gap Kemuni can exploit.
