## AI & Technology Trends

- **AI is shifting from "bolted on" to "built in"**: Industry is moving away from AI features added to legacy platforms toward AI-native tools built from scratch. Both HOA-OS and Assembly HOA exemplify this — AI is core, not a widget.

- **Autonomous agents for property management**: HOA-OS runs a daily autonomous review agent that surfaces overdue assessments, stalled violations, pending requests, and newsletter cadence — all queued for board approval. This "agentic assistant" pattern is becoming standard.

- **Yardi Breeze on "operational flattening"**: Property management orgs are eliminating middle-layer admin roles as AI handles routine work. AI excels at compliance reviews, reporting, and admin workflows. Predicts a hybrid model — AI execution, human judgment at top.
  - Source: https://www.yardibreeze.com/blog/2026/04/centralization-property-management/

- **AI document extraction gaining traction**: Template-free AI extraction from leases, rent rolls, appraisals (Lido). Auto-extracting structured data from unstructured property docs is a key use case.
  - Source: https://www.lido.app/blog/best-document-processing-for-real-estate

- **"Meaningful AI vs. hype" divide forming**: MRI Software's MD calls out "AI-washing" — properties gravitating toward tools with measurable ROI and real operational efficiency. Industry maturing past marketing buzzwords.
  - Source: https://businesstoday.me/real-estate/the-proptech-trends-set-to-reshape-uae-real-estate-in-2026/

- **Propexo (YC)**: Developer infrastructure for CRE software integrations — letting engineers build/test/launch integrations in days vs. months. Integration infrastructure becoming its own PropTech category.
  - Source: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/proptech

- **AI apartment management software**: 96 platforms tracked with rankings updated April 3, 2026. Category is extremely crowded; differentiation on AI capabilities is now table stakes.
  - Source: https://www.krowdbase.com/best-ai-apartment-management-software

## Relevant to Kemuni

- **Assembly HOA is the benchmark competitor to track**: YC-backed, 4.9-star Google rating, 26 communities in LA + SF. Two-pronged: managed service + free AI copilot (Atlas). This is the company Kemuni will be compared against. Watch their pricing, feature set, and expansion pace closely.

- **HOA-OS is the self-serve competitor**: Targets HOA boards directly, no manager needed. AI-native, 10-minute setup pitch. If Kemuni targets the same self-serve board market, this is the head-to-head. Differentiation opportunity: Kemuni's broader "community management" scope vs. HOA-OS's board-only focus.

- **"Transparency + governance" is the hot emotional trigger**: The viral HOA conflict-of-interest story shows homeowners are angry about opacity and mismanagement. Kemuni's positioning around resident transparency and governance clarity directly addresses real pain.

- **Autonomous daily agent pattern is expected**: HOA-OS's autonomous morning review agent is becoming the UX standard. Kemuni should ship a daily digest / proactive alerts feature to stay competitive.

- **AI-washing backlash is forming**: Industry is getting skeptical of "AI" labels. Kemuni should demonstrate measurable AI value (time saved, violations resolved, funds collected) — not just list AI as a feature.

- **Q1 2026 VC boom means more competitors are funded**: With $300B flowing into startups, more HOA/PropTech plays will be funded in 2026. Window to establish brand and market share is narrowing. **Move fast to launch.**

- **Integration infrastructure (Propexo) is a signal**: Developer tools for CRE integrations are getting funded. Kemuni should think early about which integrations (QuickBooks, banking, payment rails) will matter most and prioritize them.
