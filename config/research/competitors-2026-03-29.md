# Competitor Intel — 2026-03-29

## Solume (solume.ai)

**Overview:** Atlanta-based AI-first HOA management platform targeting board members and property managers. Early-stage/startup (Instagram following ~176 as of research date — small but growing).

**Key features:**
- AI-powered CC&R compliance: analyzes legal docs, detects violations, flags issues automatically
- Automated reserve studies: converts static reports into real-time dynamic financial projections
- Dues/assessment collection built-in
- Homeowner portal (community.solume.com)

**Recent news:**
- Launched AI software for HOA management (press release via StreetInsider, date unclear but recent — positioned as "first" AI-powered HOA platform)
- Actively marketing on Instagram under @solume_software; modest reach suggesting early growth stage
- No funding announcements found; appears bootstrapped or pre-seed

**Positioning:** "Helping HOA board members manage with ease" + "Making property managers more money." Direct competitor to Kemuni targeting the same self-managed/small HOA segment with AI as differentiator.

## Vantaca

**Overview:** Wilmington, NC — AI-first community association management platform targeting professional CAM companies (not self-managed boards). Clearly well-funded and scaling.

**Recent news (March 2026):**
- **March 12, 2026:** Eclipse Community Management (Dayton, OH) went live on Vantaca across 175 associations and 20,000+ doors — significant enterprise win
- Eclipse deployed Vantaca's full suite: Vantaca Pay, Vantaca IQ (analytics), HOAi (AI), and Vantaca Home (homeowner portal)
- Published blog post: "What CAM Software Buyers Get Wrong About AI" (March 2026) — thought leadership positioning, framing competitors' AI claims as marketing fluff vs. Vantaca's "real" AI

**Product suite:**
- **HOAi:** AI layer for efficiency and responsiveness
- **Vantaca IQ:** Data analytics and decision support
- **Vantaca Pay:** Integrated payments
- **Vantaca Home:** Homeowner-facing portal
- **Vantaca Vendor:** Vendor management
- "Vantaca Vision 2026" event listed on their site — likely a user conference

**Positioning:** Enterprise CAM companies (100+ associations), not self-managed boards. Emphasizing AI depth, not just AI branding.

## PayHOA

**Overview:** Founded 2018, Lexington KY by Michael Bollinger. Cloud-based, targeting self-managed HOAs. Popular for affordability and simplicity.

**Recent coverage (March 2026):**
- ManageCasa published a detailed comparison (March 3, 2026) positioning PayHOA as feature-limited and recommending their platform instead
- A "how to cancel PayHOA" page is live on Chargeback.com — signals some churn/dissatisfaction in the user base
- Mentioned in HOA software roundups as a lower-cost option lacking enterprise features

**Key features (current):**
- ACH + credit card payment processing
- Automated dues invoicing
- Basic bank integration
- Board messaging, document storage
- Work order/maintenance request tracking
- Mobile-optimized (some user complaints about limited mobile functionality)

**Positioning:** Budget-friendly, small HOAs, self-managed. **Not AI-forward.** Reviews generally positive for small communities; recurring complaint is feature gaps at scale.

**Pricing:** Not publicly confirmed for 2026, but positioned as lower-cost than Buildium/AppFolio.

## Buildium

**Overview:** RealPage-owned (acquired ~2019) property management platform. Covers residential rentals + HOAs. Cloud-based, widely used by small-to-mid-sized operators.

**Recent news (2026):**
- Published "7 of the Best AI Property Management Tools in 2026" blog post — indicates AI is now a marketing focus
- Actively referenced in AI property management tool roundups alongside AppFolio
- No major product launch announcements found in last 7 days

**Pricing (2026, confirmed):**
- Starts at ~$62/mo (flat monthly, scales by door count — NOT per-unit like AppFolio)
- Three tiers: Essential, Growth, Premium
- SaaSWorthy rating: **3.5/5** (584 ratings) — mediocre; suggests real user dissatisfaction

**Key features:**
- Workflow automations (approval triggers, updates)
- Resident portals + communication tools
- Accounting, maintenance tracking
- Integrations: HappyCo, Tenantcloud, others
- AI tools added but noted as secondary to core features

**Complaints pattern:** Onboarding complexity compared to newer tools; HOA features exist but are not the primary focus (rental-first product).

## AppFolio

**Overview:** Publicly traded (NASDAQ: APPF). Largest pure-play property management SaaS company. ~1,749 employees as of Feb 2026. Primary focus: multifamily and single-family residential — HOA is secondary.

**Recent news (March 2026):**
- **Q4 2025 earnings reported:** Free cash flow grew 30% in 2025; FCF margins near 25%
- **2026 guidance:** ~$1.1B revenue, ~26.5% adjusted operating margin (up from 24.7%)
- Stock up ~7.5% on strong earnings; analysts bullish on margin expansion
- Insight Holdings trimmed position in Q4 2025 — minor institutional signal
- **AppFolio Realm-X:** Generative AI portfolio (partnership with AWS/Amazon Nova) — handles routine management tasks and communications autonomously
- AI leasing assistant: handles prospect inquiries 24/7, self-guided tour scheduling, bulk lease renewal processing

**Pricing:**
- Per-unit model (unlike Buildium's flat fee); minimum ~$298/mo
- Premium/Plus tiers unlock AI features
- Positioned as mid-market to enterprise

**Positioning:** Deep AI integration via Realm-X, expanding "resident services" as a higher-margin revenue line. Not HOA-focused — property managers and multifamily operators are the core ICP.

## Key Takeaways

- **AI is the battlefield in 2026.** Every competitor is claiming AI. Vantaca leads messaging around "real AI vs. marketing AI." AppFolio has the most mature AI product (Realm-X / Amazon Nova partnership). Solume is the most direct AI-first HOA startup threat.

- **Solume is the closest direct competitor to Kemuni.** Same segment (HOA boards, property managers), same AI positioning, Atlanta-based startup. Small right now but the positioning overlap is high. Worth monitoring closely.

- **Vantaca is winning enterprise CAM.** The Eclipse deal (20,000+ homes, 175 associations) shows they're locking in professional management companies. Not competing directly with self-managed board software — but signals what enterprise buyers want.

- **PayHOA is vulnerable.** No visible AI investment, mobile complaints, and a competitor-published hit piece (ManageCasa) gaining SEO traction. The cancellation guide being indexed is a churn signal. This is a market gap Kemuni can exploit.

- **AppFolio is scaling aggressively** with $1.1B revenue guidance and margin expansion, but their ICP is multifamily/rental — HOA is not their focus. Buildium similarly skews rental-first. Neither is building for the community-first HOA experience Kemuni is targeting.
