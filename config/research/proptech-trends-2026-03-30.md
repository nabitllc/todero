## Relevance to Kemuni

1. **HOA governance crisis = core Kemuni opportunity.** TrueHOA's $10B litigation estimate and the wave of HOA fraud/transparency stories confirm the market pain Kemuni is targeting. Trustworthy, auditable governance tooling (voting, record-keeping, financial transparency) is in high demand — and very few platforms are solving it. Kemuni should be ready to cite the "$10B fighting HOAs" stat in positioning/marketing.

2. **Agentic AI is the new baseline expectation.** Entrata's 100+ AI agents launch sets a new benchmark for what "modern" property management software looks like. Kemuni's 30-day launch window is tight — but building AI-native from the start (vs. bolting it on) is a real differentiator. Even basic AI-assisted communications, violation detection, or document parsing would signal Kemuni is in the same generation as Entrata/Verbaflo.

3. **Regulatory compliance is a growth vector.** With Washington, California, Florida, and Arizona all changing HOA laws in 2026, compliance tooling is a real selling point — especially for board members and property managers who are legally exposed. A compliance-tracking or law-change-alert feature would be high-value, low-effort positioning leverage for Kemuni's launch.
