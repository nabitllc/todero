# Competitor Intel — 2026-03-24

## Summary
The HOA/property management software space is active this week, with AppFolio suffering a notable ~11-hour outage on March 18–19 and Vantaca continuing its customer acquisition push (Eclipse Community Management, 20,000+ homes). AI-native positioning is the dominant theme across all players — Solume leans hardest into it as a differentiator, while Buildium and AppFolio are integrating AI incrementally into established platforms. No major funding rounds or pricing changes detected for any of the five companies in the last 7 days.

No news or press coverage published in the last 7 days. No funding announcements, product releases, or notable media coverage found.

## AppFolio

**Notable events this week:**

- **March 18–19, 2026 — Major Outage:** AppFolio experienced a significant service disruption logged as ~10 hours and 52 minutes of downtime. Reports spiked on DownDetector around 7:54 AM ET on March 19. Users reported inability to access the platform app. This is a reliability vulnerability competitors can exploit in sales conversations.

**Recent context (last 2–3 weeks):**
- **March 3, 2026** — **AvidXchange** announced *Accounts Payable as a Service* partnership now embedded within the **AppFolio Performance Platform**, bringing automated AP/payment processing directly to AppFolio customers.
- Financial analysts note AppFolio's **revenue mix is shifting toward higher-margin resident-facing services** (500,000+ units on resident services). Management's 2026 targets: ~$1.1B revenue, ~26.5% adjusted operating margin.
- Stock (APPF) up ~7.5% recently on margin-rich resident services momentum.
- AppFolio pricing: starting at **$298/mo minimum** (per Leasense comparison).

## Broader Market Notes

- **ReserveFund Intelligence** (a niche HOA player) launched an AI-powered **Compliance Analysis feature** on March 20, 2026 — targeted at HOA boards and treasurers for reserve fund compliance. Small player, but signals AI is entering every corner of the HOA stack.
- **SmartWebs** published a post on March 21, 2026 about "Portfolio-Ready HOA Management Software" — content marketing targeting growing CAM companies (10–15+ associations).
- **Yardi Systems** acquired a cloud-based leasing automation platform in February 2026 to enhance digital leasing/resident experience.
- **RealPage** (Buildium's parent) made an acquisition in January 2026 (details limited in search results).
- Broader market theme: **AI-native vs AI-integrated** is the key battleground. AppFolio and Buildium are retrofitting AI into large existing platforms; Solume and newer entrants are building AI-first from scratch — same dynamic Kemuni can exploit.
- AppFolio's **11-hour outage** is a live sales opportunity: reliability and uptime can be a differentiator in Kemuni's positioning.
