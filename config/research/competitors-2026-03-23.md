## Key Takeaways

- **Vantaca is the most active this week** — closing a major 20,000-home customer win and aggressively positioning HOAi as the industry's only true "agentic AI" for community management. They're clearly going upmarket and trying to differentiate from Buildium/PayHOA.

- **AppFolio is executing on an embedded services strategy** — the AvidXchange AP automation partnership is part of a broader pattern of deepening platform lock-in via value-added services. $1.1B revenue guidance for 2026 signals they're scaling fast; the outage this week is a trust signal worth watching.

- **Buildium/RealPage is betting on AI branding** but hasn't made a product-level announcement — the "Lumina AI" mention is notable and bears further investigation. Their flat-rate $58/mo pricing makes them a credible mid-market incumbent Kemuni will need to address.

- **PayHOA is quiet this week** but raised $27.5M in mid-2024 specifically for product development. Expect new feature announcements; their self-managed HOA positioning is the most direct overlap with Kemuni's target market.

- **Solume is an early-stage direct competitor** (AI-first, self-managed HOAs, launched Nov 2025) but has minimal press presence. They're targeting the same board-member persona as Kemuni with a similar AI positioning — worth monitoring closely even though they're not generating news yet.
