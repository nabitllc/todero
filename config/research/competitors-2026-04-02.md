## 1. Solume (solume.ai)

**Overview:** AI-first HOA management platform targeting self-managed boards. Direct competitor to Kemuni. Launched in November 2025 from Atlanta, GA.

- **Platform launched Nov 2025:** Solume debuted with a full-stack AI HOA management product targeting both managed and self-managed associations. Core pitch: replace scattered spreadsheets/email with one platform + AI document interpreter. ([completeaitraining.com](https://completeaitraining.com/news/solume-launches-ai-hoa-management-platform-with-real-time/), [kingnewswire.com](https://kingnewswire.com/solume-launches-ai-software-for-easier-hoa-management-25110836539/))
- **Key features at launch:** Dynamic real-time reserve study management (updates as actuals change), AI assistant that reads governing documents and guides procedural decisions, architectural review workflows, dues/expense/vendor payment integration, built-in communications. ([satellitesview.com](https://satellitesview.com/press-release/2025-11-15/26290/solume-launches-ai-driven-reserve-study-management-for-hoas))
- **Target audience:** Volunteer-run boards that need governance guidance without a professional management company — same ICP as Kemuni.
- **Differentiator messaging:** Empowering boards to take a more active, confident role; reducing reliance on management companies.
- **No recent funding announcements or major product updates found for March/April 2026.** Appears still in early-growth phase; no press in last 7 days.
- **Free trial available** per their site (community.solume.com).

## Key Takeaways for Kemuni

1. **Solume is the closest direct threat in the self-managed HOA AI space.** They launched in Nov 2025 with nearly identical positioning (AI for volunteer boards, document interpretation, reserve management). Kemuni needs a clear differentiation story — especially on ease of onboarding, pricing, and community-specific features like voting/elections that Solume may not emphasize.

2. **PayHOA owns the "affordable self-managed" category but has no AI narrative.** This is Kemuni's biggest opportunity: PayHOA users are loyal to simplicity and price, but they're not getting AI-powered guidance or modern UX. A side-by-side showing AI-powered governance + competitive pricing could convert PayHOA users.

3. **Vantaca and AppFolio are moving upmarket, not down.** Both are doubling down on enterprise CAM companies, not self-managed boards. Their agentic AI features are impressive but overkill and overpriced for a 50-unit self-managed HOA. Kemuni should emphasize "built for self-managed, not adapted from enterprise."

4. **AI is now table stakes in the HOA software pitch.** Every competitor is invoking AI in demos. Kemuni needs concrete, demo-ready AI features (document parsing, budget guidance, meeting automation) — not just a roadmap promise. Vantaca's blog post signals buyers are getting skeptical of generic "AI-powered" claims; Kemuni should lead with specific use cases and outcomes.

5. **Buildium's pricing complexity and rental-first design is a wedge.** Self-managed HOA boards choosing Buildium are making a compromise. Kemuni can win on fit: purpose-built for HOAs, easier onboarding, no rental-world baggage, and HOA-specific workflows (violations, ARC requests, assessments, elections) front and center from day one.
