# PropTech & HOA Software Trends — 2026-03-24

## This Week's Top Trends

- **AI-native PropTech is pulling VC away from legacy SaaS**: PropTech VC hit $16.7B in 2025 (up 68% YoY), with AI companies capturing ~$4.5B and growing faster. Investors are explicitly shifting away from traditional SaaS models toward AI-first platforms. Three new PropTech unicorns since mid-2024 are all AI-native. *(BisNow/AllWork.Space, March 17–18, 2026)*
- **Fannie Mae + Freddie Mac overhaul condo/HOA financing rules**: FHFA released major updates (LL-2026-03) on March 18, including raising mandatory HOA reserve allocations from 10% to 15% of annual budget, revising insurance standards, and expanding condo financing access. This is a significant regulatory shift affecting all community associations. *(Scotsman Guide, Newsweek, HOA Leadership Network, March 19–21, 2026)*
- **KeyPath wins NAR/SXSW PropTech Startup Showdown (March 13–14)**: An AI-powered fintech that helps renters build tokenized equity in properties won top honors, signaling investor and industry appetite for renter-to-ownership conversion tools. *(NAR Magazine, Forbes, March 14–18, 2026)*
- **Blockchain enters HOA/community management**: My Green Condo (MGC) received a U.S. patent (No. 12,443,952) for blockchain-based community governance and launched "Association as a Service Organization" — calling for management companies to co-develop a decentralized association management standard. *(PRNewswire/AI Journal, March 18, 2026)*
- **PropTech market projected to reach $77.98B by 2032** at a CAGR of 15.7%, with construction tech the fastest-growing segment at 45% CAGR. Global market currently valued at ~$44.59B. *(MarkNtel Advisors via PRNewswire, March 18, 2026; TechBullion, March 17, 2026)*

## New Product Launches / Features

- **CINC Systems — Payables+** (launched Feb 26, 2026): AI-powered accounts payable automation for community association management (CAM) companies. Integrates AP automation and secure payments in a single platform; targets CAM companies looking to cut costs and scale. *(PRNewswire, Feb 26, 2026)*
- **Braiin Limited (Nasdaq: BRAI) — AI Property Management Platform** (launched March 11, 2026): Automates listings, tenant screening, inspections, maintenance workflows, and financial management via a unified digital operating system for rental portfolios. *(Morningstar/AccessNewswire, March 11, 2026)*
- **My Green Condo — MGCOne Platform** (announced March 18, 2026): Blockchain-based decentralized community association management platform with a patented architecture. Inviting founding partners to co-develop. *(PRNewswire, March 18, 2026)*
- **Vantaca** published positioning content (March 2026) describing 3 types of HOA software in 2026 and claiming companies are cutting operational costs 60–70% using their platform.
- **Effortless HOA** and **ZipDo** published updated 2026 buyer's guides covering platforms from PayHOA to AppFolio — reflecting a market with increasing consumer pressure for differentiation.

## AI in PropTech

- **58% of proactive property managers** now using AI in operations (2026 industry reports via coradvisors.net). Predictive maintenance adoption is accelerating.
- **Buildium's "Lumina AI"** highlighted as a leading AI tool for property managers focused on security and trust (as of Feb 2026).
- AI use cases dominating: predictive maintenance, tenant screening automation, lease review, dynamic pricing, financial reporting, and maintenance workflow automation.
- **Trend: Real estate firms building their own AI tools** — once unrealistic, this is now possible for large operators with newer AI infrastructure, raising competitive risk for software vendors. *(BisNow, March 17, 2026)*
- **AppFolio** highlighted as building a connected AI ecosystem; Lula (maintenance partner) published March 2026 commentary on the trend. *(lula.life, March 2026)*
- **Key AI application in HOA/CAM**: Accounts payable automation (CINC's Payables+), financial reporting, reserve study modeling, and communications.

## Market Signals

- **Global PropTech market**: ~$44.59B in 2026, projected to reach $77.98B by 2032 (15.7% CAGR). *(MarkNtel via PRNewswire, March 18, 2026)*
- **AI-native companies are growing faster** than SaaS incumbents and taking an increasing share of new VC. "The long-term winners are unclear" — some VCs believe established platforms with deep data could hold or grow stronger; others expect AI to replace large parts of SaaS. *(BisNow, AllWork.Space, March 17–18, 2026)*
- **Fannie/Freddie reserve rule change** (reserve minimum raised to 15%) will force HOAs to reckon with reserve management software more urgently — creates an opening for tools with strong financial planning/compliance features.
- **HOA software market (2026)**: Ranges from simple communication tools to full-featured management suites. Key players include CINC Systems, Vantaca, AppFolio, Buildium, TownSq, PayHOA, Effortless HOA. Self-managed HOA segment is growing. *(effortlesshoa.com, zipdo.co, wildapricot.com)*
- **VC selectivity increasing**: February 2026 showed fewer deals but larger check sizes vs. January — suggesting a maturing, consolidating market. *(CRETI, March 2026)*

## Relevance to Kemuni

The regulatory tailwind from the Fannie/Freddie reserve mandate (minimum raised to 15%) is directly actionable for Kemuni — HOA boards will need better reserve tracking and financial compliance tools, creating near-term buyer urgency that Kemuni can address in messaging. The market is clearly bifurcating between all-in-one AI-native platforms and niche tools; launching as a unified community + property management solution with AI-first positioning aligns with where VC and buyers are moving, but Kemuni should define a clear wedge (e.g., self-managed HOAs, smaller communities, compliance-forward features) to avoid competing head-on with CINC Systems or AppFolio on day one. The MGCOne blockchain play and KeyPath tokenized equity angle are worth monitoring — both signal that the "community ownership" narrative is gaining traction, which may be a future differentiator for Kemuni's community angle.
