## Market Shifts & Analyst Notes

### Property Management Software Market Size
- **Overall PM software market: $7.28 billion in 2026** (The Business Research Company, via Baselane)
- **Residential PM software**: growing from $1.139B (2025) → $1.831B (2032) at **7.2% CAGR** (Industry Today)
- **Overall property management market** (services + software): projected to reach **$40.7 billion by 2031** (OpenPR, March 27, 2026)
- **AMS (Association Management Software) market**: $2.61B (2025) → **$2.97B in 2026**, ~13.9% YoY growth (iMIS blog)

### HOA Software Market
- The HOA software segment is growing driven by: digital community management demand, online payment adoption, and compliance needs
- Market categorized by enterprise size (SMEs vs large) and application type — SME segment is underserved and growing fast
- Key competitors in HOA space (2026 rankings): **HOA Express, PayHOA, CINC Systems, Vantaca, AppFolio, Buildium, TownSq, Effortless HOA, DoorLoop, Neighborly Software**

### VC Trend: AI-Native Beats SaaS
- VCs are explicitly moving away from traditional property management SaaS toward AI-native platforms
- Thesis: AI companies run leaner, build proprietary datasets from operations, and improve over time — compounding moat
- "Established platforms with deep data could hold or grow stronger" — but only if they successfully integrate AI
- Real estate firms are now exploring building custom AI models rather than just buying software (BisNow, March 17, 2026)

### Agentic AI is the New Bar
- Vantaca's blog (widely circulated in CAM circles) breaks down 3 AI tiers in HOA/CAM software:
  1. **Prompt-based AI** (chatbots, writing assistants — passive)
  2. **Embedded AI shortcuts** (accelerate specific tasks)
  3. **Agentic AI** (autonomous end-to-end workflows — the only one that changes cost structure)
- Market narrative: only agentic AI meaningfully reduces headcount needs for management companies
- HOAi described as "first agentic AI workforce purpose-built for community association management"

### Self-Managed HOA Segment — Underserved but Active
- Growing segment: **self-managed HOAs** (boards without professional management companies)
- Pain points most cited: dues collection/late payments, architectural review backlogs, violation tracking, financial reporting for boards
- Platforms specifically targeting this: HOA Express, PayHOA, Effortless HOA, GatherGrove
- Pricing benchmarks: $58/mo (up to 20 units) → $338/mo (500+ units) for self-managed tier

## Summary — 3-5 Key Takeaways Most Relevant to Kemuni

### 1. 🔥 Agentic AI Is the New Competitive Table Stakes — But Mostly for Enterprise
The entire CAM/HOA industry is racing to claim "agentic AI." Entrata's 100-agent launch, CINC's Payables+, AppFolio's Realm-X, and Vantaca's HOAi are all targeting **professional management companies** managing thousands of units. **The self-managed and small community segment is largely ignored by these enterprise players.** Kemuni launching here has a real window — boards and resident-run associations don't need enterprise-grade agentic AI. They need **simple, modern, affordable tools that just work.**

### 2. 💡 The Self-Managed HOA Segment Is Growing and Underserved
The HOA software market is growing at ~14% YoY, but the dominant products (CINC, Vantaca, AppFolio) are built for and priced for professional management companies. Self-managed segment alternatives (PayHOA, HOA Express, Effortless HOA) are lightweight but thin on features. **Kemuni can own this niche** if positioned as the modern, community-first platform for boards that don't have a management company — or don't want one.

### 3. 📊 The Market Is Massive and Validated — Good for Investor Conversations
PropTech investment hit $16.7B in 2025 (up 68%). The AMS/HOA software market is $2.97B in 2026 growing at ~14% YoY. Property management software overall is $7.28B and growing. **This is not a niche — it's a legitimized, venture-backed market.** Kemuni's pitch deck should reference these numbers.

### 4. ⚡ Resident Communication + Payments Are Still the Biggest Adoption Drivers
Across all research, the features with highest adoption and most immediate ROI for HOA boards are: **dues/payment collection, resident communications, and document management.** These should be Kemuni's MVP pillars — not AI agents or predictive maintenance. Get those three right, charge fairly, and you have product-market fit.

### 5. 🎯 The AI "Expectation Gap" Is an Opportunity
Vantaca's buyer guide is telling buyers that most "AI" in CAM software is smoke and mirrors — only agentic AI changes cost structures. But that's an enterprise conversation. For Kemuni's target (self-managed boards, smaller communities), **the opportunity is to lead with simplicity and transparency**, not AI buzzwords. Be the antidote to the complexity that CINC and AppFolio bring. A clean product that works on day one will differentiate sharply in this noisy AI-hype cycle.
