## Summary

1. **Vantaca is the most active competitor right now.** Major client win (Eclipse, 20K+ homes), annual user conference with product announcements, aggressive AI positioning, and content marketing blitz — all in March 2026. They're pushing hard on enterprise CAM deals.

2. **AppFolio is the financial powerhouse.** $951M FY2025 revenue, guiding to $1.1B in 2026. Their Realm-X AI suite (powered by AWS Amazon Nova) is the most technically credible AI play in the space. They're also monetizing residents directly (insurance, internet) — a revenue model most competitors aren't replicating.

3. **Solume is the closest analog to Kemuni** — AI-first, HOA-focused, small footprint, no notable funding or press. Likely early-stage. Worth monitoring closely for positioning overlap.

4. **PayHOA and Buildium are stable but not innovating visibly.** PayHOA owns the self-managed small HOA niche. Buildium is solid mid-market but getting squeezed by comparison content pushing cheaper/AI-native alternatives. Neither made notable announcements this month.

5. **AI is the central battleground.** Every major player is claiming AI leadership. Vantaca and AppFolio are the most credible; PayHOA and Buildium are lagging. Kemuni has a window to differentiate on genuine AI utility vs. AI marketing.
