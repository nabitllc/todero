# PropTech / HOA Software Trends — 2026-04-01

## Bottom line
There were **not many clearly viral HOA-specific signals in the last 7 days**. The stronger signals were adjacent: AI moving from marketing copy into back-office property operations, operator demand for cleaner accounting/AP workflows, affordability pressure around HOA fees, and compliance pressure pushing associations toward better resident/document portals.

## Signals from the last 7 days

### 1) AI is shifting from chatbot hype to operational automation
- **RealPage** published a launch-style push around an **AI Operations Agent** for multifamily operations. It is positioned as an always-on workflow layer for move-outs, renewals, ledger updates, audits, charges/payments, and exception handling.
- A separate **NewMark Merrill** piece says owners/operators are actively testing AI across leasing, legal review, facilities, accounting, and research, with firms now creating **formal AI evaluation processes** instead of random tool trials.
- Read as a market signal: operators want AI that removes repetitive admin work and protects revenue/compliance — not just resident-facing chat.
- Sources:
  - https://www.realpage.com/blog/agentic-ai-efficiently-managing-multifamily-property-operations/
  - https://newmarkmerrill.com/proptech-at-scale-how-real-estate-teams-are-vetting-ai-without-getting-overwhelmed/

### 2) Accounting and receipt capture are becoming a sharper wedge
- **RentRedi** pushed survey data saying **59% of landlords** cite expense categorization as their top tax-prep headache, and that **78% of tax-prep friction** traces back to expense ingestion/categorization.
- **RealPage** also emphasized property-management-specific accounting complexity: property/unit-level tracking, tenant ledgers, trust accounting, and reporting depth.
- For SMB operators, this suggests demand for simpler workflows around **receipts, charges, approvals, property-level coding, and audit readiness**.
- Sources:
  - https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/31/3265283/0/en/AI-can-now-Eliminate-78-of-Tax-Prep-Headaches-for-Landlords.html
  - https://www.realpage.com/blog/what-is-property-management-accounting/

### 3) AP/invoice automation is emerging as practical “must-have” infrastructure
- **Vergo** highlighted the gap between core PM/accounting systems like AppFolio and the messy upstream invoice workflow: intake, coding, approval routing, duplicate detection, mobile approvals, and audit trail.
- This is not an HOA-specific launch, but it reflects where operators are spending attention: less on shiny dashboards, more on **financial workflow reliability**.
- Source:
  - https://www.getvergo.com/learn/what-ap-automation-tools-integrate-with-appfolio-for-real-estate-companies

### 4) Board/resident visibility remains a real retention issue in HOA software switching
- **Vantaca** published a software-switching piece centered on a familiar industry fear: losing accounts during platform migration.
- The most useful detail was that managers reportedly won boards over by promising **better visibility and communication**, then running **board-specific webinars** ahead of go-live.
- That reinforces a product truth: in HOA/community management, software wins when it clearly improves the board/resident experience, not just internal admin.
- Source:
  - https://www.vantaca.com/blog/how-to-switch-software-without-losing-a-single-account

### 5) Compliance pressure is still pushing communities toward portals and better document handling
- A March 2026 **Florida Condo & HOA Law Blog** update highlighted that Florida condo associations with 25+ units must now maintain a **website/web portal** with required documents posted.
- That is region-specific, but it matters: compliance is creating demand for structured document access, owner self-service, and better division of responsibility between associations and management companies.
- Source:
  - https://www.floridacondohoalawblog.com/community-update-march-2026/

### 6) Market pressure: HOA fees are becoming more visible in affordability conversations
- **U.S. News** framed HOA fees as a major part of the 2026 affordability discussion, noting that nearly **44% of homes for sale in 2025 had HOA fees**.
- This is not software news, but it matters strategically: when fees are under scrutiny, boards/managers need better resident communication around budgets, assessments, maintenance, and value delivered.
- Source:
  - https://money.usnews.com/loans/mortgages/articles/hoa-fees-vs-mortgage-rates-which-is-actually-driving-the-2026-affordability-crisis

### 7) Notable adjacent launch: AI workflow tooling for fragmented property processes
- In UK proptech, **XchangeHero** launched with CRM + chain-management + AI inbox features to reduce transaction failures.
- It is not HOA software, but the relevant pattern is strong: a vertical workflow product wins by **aggregating fragmented communication and surfacing exceptions automatically**.
- That pattern maps well to HOA/community ops (violations, maintenance, approvals, board comms, vendor follow-up).

## What seems to be trending underneath the headlines
1. **Operational AI > novelty AI** — vendors are selling automation for ledgers, audits, renewals, invoice handling, and exception management.
2. **Accounting pain is still under-served** — receipt capture, categorization, AP approval routing, and audit trails keep showing up.
3. **Resident/board trust is a feature** — visibility, communication, and document access remain switching triggers.
4. **Compliance can drive adoption** — especially where laws force portals, records, or posting requirements.
5. **Economic pressure raises the bar** — as HOA fees get scrutinized, software needs to help managers justify costs and communicate clearly.

## Why this matters for Kemuni
- Kemuni should lean into **resident-facing clarity + back-office execution**, not just one or the other.
- The strongest opportunity looks like **simple, trustworthy workflows** for communication, documents, approvals, payments/accounting handoffs, and issue follow-up.
- If Kemuni can make boards and residents feel informed while also reducing manual admin for SMB operators, that is more aligned with current market pull than broad “AI for property management” messaging.
- A smart positioning angle: **“clearer community operations with less admin overhead”** — then selectively layer AI into the tedious parts, especially categorization, routing, summaries, and exception detection.
