## AI in PropTech

- **Agentic AI is the dominant narrative** — multiple pieces this week on the shift from "automation" (if/then rules) to true AI (contextual decision-making). Property managers being pushed to understand the difference. AppFolio is positioning itself as "the industry's only real estate Performance Platform" with data + AI + workflows in one system.  
  → [Jurny Blog](https://blog.jurny.com/pms-automation-vs.-true-ai-what-property-managers-need-to-know-in-2026) | [Lula](https://lula.life/articles/the-future-of-property-management-technology)

- **Predictive maintenance maturing** — shift from time-based to condition-based maintenance now mainstream in property management. OxMaint guide notes this as the "most significant capability evolution 2023–2026."  
  → [OxMaint](https://oxmaint.com/industries/property-management/complete-guide-property-maintenance-management-software-2026)

- **AI for leasing, scheduling, and pricing** — embedded in major platforms. AppFolio, Buildium, and Yardi all adding AI-adjacent features. Every platform now claims "AI" in marketing — making differentiation harder.

- **Agentic AI workflows** — real estate firms exploring custom-built AI models (opportunity analysis, underwriting, workflow coordination). Previously unrealistic; now achievable for large operators.  
  → [Antier Solutions](https://www.antiersolutions.com/blogs/the-complete-guide-to-ai-in-real-estate-investment-and-operations-in-2026/)

- **86% of property management software buyers rate online payments as top priority** (Capterra 2026 data) — second only to accounting. Simple signals: don't underestimate payment UX.  
  → [ManageCasa](https://managecasa.com/articles/best-payment-software-for-property-management)

## Signal vs. Noise

- 🟢 **SIGNAL: AI-native > AI-bolted-on.** The VC shift is real — $4.5B of $16.7B going to AI-native companies, growing 42% YoY. Platforms that build AI into their core architecture (not as a feature layer) are winning both investment and customers. Kemuni should build data/AI-first, not add AI later.

- 🟢 **SIGNAL: Self-managed HOA is an underserved wedge.** HOA Start's adoption announcement + YouTube HOA content + r/fuckHOA activity all point to a large, frustrated, underserved population. Professional management software (CINC, Vantaca, etc.) ignores them. This is exactly Kemuni's opening.

- 🟡 **NOISE: Blockchain for HOAs.** My Green Condo's blockchain announcement is a PR play. Decentralized association management standards have zero adoption momentum. Ignore.

- 🟢 **SIGNAL: Payments are non-negotiable.** 86% of property management software buyers rank online payments as top priority (Capterra). Any MVP without a payments story is DOA with buyers.

- 🟡 **WATCH: Consolidation via PE.** Communitas joining PHM Group is one data point, but the broader pattern of PE rolling up HOA/community management companies continues. This eventually commoditizes the management layer — opportunity for software to outlast services plays.
