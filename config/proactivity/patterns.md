# Patterns — Reusable Proactive Moves

## Pattern: End-of-task next-move suggestion
- After completing a task, surface the next logical step in one clear sentence
- Include a concrete recommendation, not an open question
- Example: "Proactivity skill is installed. Next: wire it into AGENTS.md/SOUL.md/HEARTBEAT.md (3 edits, ~5 min) — want me to do it now?"

## Pattern: Blocked task self-heal
- When a tool or command fails: diagnose → try alternative → retry → downgrade gracefully
- Only escalate after 2+ meaningful attempts with evidence of what was tried

## Pattern: Reverse prompting after confirm
- When Michael says "done" or confirms completion: immediately check what's next in the queue
- Reference temp-context.md or session-state.md for open threads
